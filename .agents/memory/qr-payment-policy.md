---
name: Guest QR payment policy
description: Guest payment methods, online-only tips, configured recipient and fiscal separation requested by the user.
---

Guest QR must offer LiqPay checkout or a request to pay cash at the cashier. A cash request is not a completed payment and must not close the bill.

Before online payment offer optional tips of 10%, 15%, or 20%, with an explicit no-tip choice. Tips go to the waiter's card configured in the cabinet. Do not include tips in the venue's fiscal sales amount.

Tips are offered only with online payment, not with cash.

**Why:** The user requested the two methods, those three percentages, the configured recipient card, and fiscal separation, and explicitly selected «Чайові лише при онлайн-оплаті».

**How to apply:** Keep meal settlement and tip payout amounts separate. Never interpret a guest cash request as collected cash, or a successful meal payment as a confirmed tip payout. Excluding tips from the venue's sales receipt is not a claim that the recipient's income is tax exempt.
