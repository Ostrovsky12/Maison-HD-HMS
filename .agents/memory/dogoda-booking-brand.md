---
name: DOGODA booking brand scope
description: Guest booking branding is specific to DOGODA, not a platform-wide redesign.
---

The user requires the redesign of the **guest-facing booking page**, including photo and video galleries, to follow the DOGODA brandbook. «Але це ми робимо виключно для DOGODA». They also want these appearance settings available in the account.

**Why:** The booking module also serves other hotels; the user explicitly limited this brand treatment to DOGODA.

**How to apply:** Keep DOGODA styling tenant-specific and configurable. Do not apply DOGODA colors, fonts, copy, or media to other tenants, the overall Maison HD admin interface, or unrelated apps. A design preview does not authorize opening Servio sales or creating real reservations.

The guest module must open directly with dates and guests, including adding children. Then show available rooms/houses with photos/video, descriptions and prices; next collect guest details; finally show confirmation and payment. Do not add a hero or a separate accommodation showcase before the date search.

**Why:** The user explicitly said the first two introductory screens are unnecessary and the module should start at date selection.

**How to apply:** Treat DOGODA booking as a sequential transaction, not a landing page. Keep accommodation media with the post-search selection, even when revisiting the brand design.

Center the guest page headings, labels, and descriptive copy on both desktop and mobile. On mobile, remove decorative English copy, keep arrival/departure side by side, and keep adults/children side by side.

**Why:** The user first requested this mobile arrangement, then explicitly asked «відцентруй всі написи, щоб гарно виглядало на сторінці».

**How to apply:** Keep the composition DOGODA-specific; center descriptive text but preserve readable input text and aligned amounts.

Do not display a single arrival/departure time for DOGODA's whole property. Show its complete configured establishment name, without an ellipsis.

**Why:** The user said the hotel and houses have different arrival/departure times and asked for the full establishment name.

**How to apply:** Omit shared time claims in the guest search presentation; do not change operational times in Servio or backend settings as a styling fix.

Remove the upper guest-facing viewing/payment-readiness strip.

**Why:** The user explicitly asked to remove it immediately so it is not forgotten.

**How to apply:** Do not restore the prominent strip during later payment-readiness work. Preserve disabled checkout safeguards and explain missing payment readiness in context at checkout instead.

Guest-facing DOGODA copy must not show the internal tariff name «NR Site» or tell guests administrators may call them. The user states that the selectable additional services are free. Wishes copy should emphasize service and an ideal stay, with only a brief note that some wishes may need additional payment.

**Why:** The user said «Доп послуги-вони безкоштовні» and «Ми про сервіс».

**How to apply:** Keep the internal Servio NR Site selection intact; separate provider identifiers from guest display copy, including confirmation emails. Do not imply that free selectable services have a surcharge, or promise that every custom wish is free.
