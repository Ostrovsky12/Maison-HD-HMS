Build a premium mobile-first interactive walking map and audio guide web app for a countryside hospitality complex called DOGODA in Dzvinkove, Kyiv Oblast, Ukraine.

Core concept:
This is not just a map. It is a premium digital nature companion for guests staying at DOGODA.
The emotional tone must feel calm, intelligent, immersive, elegant, quiet, and deeply connected to nature.

Important location context:
Use DOGODA as the starting point for route generation and nearby exploration:
DOGODA, Berezova Street 4, Dzvinkove, Kyiv Oblast, Ukraine.

The app should support:
- internal territory walking
- nearby nature exploration
- local discovery around Dzvinkove
- educational cards about trees, plants, mushrooms, landscapes, and local points of interest
- premium audio guide mode
- admin panel
- bilingual support (Ukrainian / English)

CRITICAL INTERNET RESEARCH REQUIREMENT:
Inside the app architecture and seed logic, include a module or service layer that can ingest and manage externally researched places near Dzvinkove starting from DOGODA as the anchor point.

The system must:
1. Suggest nearby walking destinations, scenic points, quiet spots, horse-related locations, river valley viewpoints, forest edges, and other real-world attractions around Dzvinkove.
2. Use publicly available internet information to propose these locations.
3. Mark each discovered location with a confidence/status label:
   - Verified from source
   - Needs manual review
   - AI-suggested draft
4. Never invent “factual” attractions without marking them as unverified if source confidence is weak.
5. Allow the admin to approve, edit, reject, or hide any AI-proposed route or attraction.
6. Use DOGODA as the default route origin.
7. Prioritize nature, silence, scenic atmosphere, soft recreation, and premium countryside experience over commercial tourist clutter.

Known local context to reflect in UX and seed assumptions:
- DOGODA is in Dzvinkove, Kyiv Oblast
- the area is associated with forest landscapes
- the Irpin river valley is nearby
- Golden Horse equestrian club is nearby and may be used as a reference point if helpful
- the product should explore and suggest meaningful nearby routes from DOGODA outward

IMPORTANT SAFETY + TRUST RULE:
Because local internet data may be incomplete, the app must separate:
- confirmed local points
- suggested points for human review
The admin panel must make this easy to manage.

DOGODA is a quiet retreat. The app must feel like a premium extension of the guest experience.
