---
name: Guest QR payment policy
description: Guest payment methods, online-only tips, configured recipient and fiscal separation requested by the user.
---

Guest QR must offer LiqPay checkout or a request to pay cash at the cashier. A cash request is not a completed payment and must not close the bill.

Запит «Розрахунок готівкою» має надходити на касу Poster, як і онлайн-замовлення. Сповіщення лише в Maison HD не виконує цю вимогу.

**Why:** Користувач уточнив: «Розрахунок готівкою - інформація має приходити на касу постер як і онлайн замовлення».

**How to apply:** Deliver the request against the existing shared bill with the service point and amount. Do not create a duplicate online order or mark cash as received to simulate a cashier notification. Report delivery to Poster only after that delivery is confirmed.

Before online payment offer optional tips of 10%, 15%, or 20%, with an explicit no-tip choice. Tips go to the waiter's card configured in the cabinet. Do not include tips in the venue's fiscal sales amount.

Tips are offered only with online payment, not with cash.

**Why:** The user requested the two methods, those three percentages, the configured recipient card, and fiscal separation, and explicitly selected «Чайові лише при онлайн-оплаті».

**How to apply:** Keep meal settlement and tip payout amounts separate. Never interpret a guest cash request as collected cash, or a successful meal payment as a confirmed tip payout. Excluding tips from the venue's sales receipt is not a claim that the recipient's income is tax exempt.

Acquiring credentials and a saved recipient card do not establish permission to perform card payouts.

**Why:** LiqPay documents account-to-card payouts as a separately connected banking capability, while payment splitting requires registered LiqPay merchant recipients rather than arbitrary personal cards: https://www.liqpay.ua/en/doc/api/transferring_to_card?tab=0 and https://www.liqpay.ua/en/doc/api/internet_acquiring/splitting .

**How to apply:** Confirm the provider-approved payout arrangement before collecting tips intended for a personal card. A Poster QR feature is a product reference, not proof that Maison HD has the same payment-provider contract or terminal capabilities.
